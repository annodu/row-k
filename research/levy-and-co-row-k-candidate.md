# ROW K candidate research: Levy & Co

Checked: 10 October 2026

## Recommendation

**Add. High-confidence candidate.** Levy & Co is a strong fit for the directory's short-hair and natural-hair audience. Its own website says it works with all hair types and textures, uses gender-neutral pricing, and is an openly LGBTQ+ friendly space. Google has also published a profile in which founder Syreeta Levy says she personally set the business's Women-Led and LGBTQ+ attributes on its Google Business Profile.

Main limitation: this is a barbershop for short hair only. It does not offer blow-drying, colouring, long-hair styling, or general hairdressing.
